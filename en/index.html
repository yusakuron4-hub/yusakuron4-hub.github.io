Minesweeper FAQ

How do you play Minesweeper?

Open squares; a number tells you how many mines touch that square. Use the numbers to work out where the mines are, flag them, and open every safe square to win. Your first click in Minesweeper Speed is always safe.

What is no-guess Minesweeper?

In no-guess mode every board can be solved with logic alone, so you never face a pure 50/50 guess. Winning depends only on your reasoning and speed.

Is Minesweeper Speed free?

Yes. It is free to download and play. It shows ads, which you can remove with an in-app purchase.

Can I play without installing?

Yes. Online Minesweeper runs right in your browser on desktop and mobile. Get the app for Adventure, the Daily Race and global leaderboards.

Can I compete with other players?

Yes. Sign in to Google Play Games for global leaderboards on Beginner, Intermediate, Expert, the Daily Race and Adventure stars, plus 25 achievements. Everyone plays the same Daily Race board each day.
