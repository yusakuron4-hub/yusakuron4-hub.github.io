How to play Minesweeper

  1. Goal: open every square that is not a mine. Hit a mine and the game is over.
  2. Numbers: a number tells you how many mines touch that square among its 8 neighbors (including diagonals).
  3. Blank squares have no neighboring mines and automatically open the area around them.
  4. Flags: mark squares you are sure are mines so you don’t click them by accident. The counter shows mines minus flags.
  5. Chording: when a number already has that many flags around it, click the number to open all its other neighbors at once. A wrong flag means a mine – careful!

Minesweeper tips for beginners

1. Look for satisfied numbers

If a number already has as many flags as its value, every other hidden neighbor is safe – open them or just chord the number.

2. Hidden squares = number? All mines

If a “3” has exactly three hidden neighbors left, all three are mines. Flag them.

3. The 1-2-1 pattern

A row of 1-2-1 along a row of hidden squares: the squares facing the 1s are mines and the one facing the 2 is safe.

5. Want to be faster? Flag less, chord more

Fast players only flag mines they need for chording, then open large areas by clicking numbers. It saves a lot of clicks.

What is no-guess Minesweeper?

Classic Minesweeper sometimes leaves you with a 50/50: one of two squares is a mine and there is no way to tell which. With No-guess on, the board is generated after your first click and checked by a solver, so it is always solvable with pure logic. Winning depends only on your reasoning and speed.

Minesweeper FAQ

Do I need to download anything?

No. Minesweeper runs right in your browser on desktop and mobile – no download, install or sign-up.

What do the numbers mean?

A number shows how many mines are in the 8 squares around it. A 1 means exactly one neighboring mine; a blank square means none and opens the area around it.

Can I hit a mine on the first click?

No. The square you click first and its 8 neighbors are always mine-free.

What is no-guess mode?

In no-guess mode every board can be solved with logic alone, so you never face a pure 50/50 guess.

How do I flag on a phone?

Long-press a square, or switch the mode below the board to “Flag” and tap. On desktop, right-click to flag.

Are my best times saved?

Yes, in your browser on this device. They are removed if you clear browsing data. To compete worldwide, get the Minesweeper Speed app with global leaderboards.

Is this the same as classic Windows Minesweeper?

Yes – the same rules and the same Beginner, Intermediate and Expert board sizes, plus a guaranteed safe first click.
